## The brief

A CV says where a designer has worked. It says nothing about how they think. Most of that lives in years of posts, captions and colours that nobody reads end to end. Taste Suite does, and files what it finds.

## The design

The landing is loud on purpose: a black page, a lime accent, a marquee of the brands on the board and headlines set in a wide, heavy Archivo. It reads like the work it describes. Two sample files sit up front, each with its counts, from companies designed for to the span of the archive.

Each designer gets a profile and a full taste file on its own URL. The profile is quieter: who they are, how they design, their signature moves, what to steal, and when their taste is wrong for you.

> Nothing is written until the split has been seen. Every number in the file can be traced to a post.

## The details

- **Four steps, shown plainly.** Harvest, classify, read, ship: the page explains how a file is made before asking anyone to request one.
- **A directory that grows.** Every filed designer joins a searchable directory, filtered by discipline.
- **Honest about fit.** Each profile ends with when that designer's taste is the wrong choice.
- **Dark and light.** A switch in the corner swaps the whole site between the two themes.

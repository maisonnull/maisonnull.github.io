## The brief

At lunch, people order from their desk with ten minutes to spare. They want their usual bowl, a clear price and an honest calorie count, and they want to know which outlet is open and how long pickup takes. Everything else gets in the way.

## The design

The home page leads with the signature bowls, each showing its real price, calories and protein from the live menu. A strip under the hero shows an outlet that is open right now and the pickup time.

The builder is five steps: base, greens, proteins, toppings and dressing. Every ingredient is a real photograph. As you pick, the bowl beside the list fills with what you chose, and the calories, protein and price update with it.

> Nutrition you can trust. No rounding up, no mystery macros.

Rewards, catering and outlets follow the same plain layout, so a returning customer always knows where they are.

## The details

- **Lime means go.** The bright lime is kept for the main action on every screen.
- **Numbers first.** Calories, protein and price are set large, because they are what people compare.
- **Fast on a phone.** The menu collapses below 1100 pixels and scrolling stays smooth on a mid-range laptop.

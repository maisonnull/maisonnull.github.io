## The brief

Song Lang Garden has served Katong for years, but online it had only a Facebook page. People nearby search for "plants for an HDB corridor" or "orchids near me" and never find it. The site had to make a small nursery feel worth the trip, and help people choose the right plant before they arrive.

## The design

Every photograph is of the nursery itself: the frontage, the orchid house, the shade house and the wall of pots. An illustrated sketch of the nursery leads a guided walk through it, stop by stop.

Shopping starts from where the plant will live: balcony, corridor, indoors, garden or office. A four-question picker narrows that to a few plants from its shelves, and each plant has a care guide written for Singapore, from monsoon months to corridor light.

> Every plant is honest about what it needs.

The visit section shows whether the nursery is open right now, with directions by bus, MRT and car.

## The details

- **Fraunces with a soft italic.** One italic word per headline gives the site a hand-lettered warmth.
- **One bold accent.** Bloom orange is kept for the hero word, the tour progress and the active map zone.
- **No invented prices.** Plants show an indicative price band and say to ask in store.

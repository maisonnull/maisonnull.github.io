## The brief

Baccarat Rouge 540 is one of the most recognised fragrances in the world, but online it is a product page like any other: a bottle, a price, an add-to-bag button. The concept asks what a flagship boutique would feel like if it were built for someone who has never smelled it.

## The design

The page opens on the red Extrait key visual, with the name set large in Bodoni Moda. The fragrance is then told as three auras, air, mineral and fire, each a full-screen colour wash with the real notes the house lists. The scroll ends where it began, on the bottle.

A slider turns the Eau de parfum into the Extrait. The bottle, notes and price cross-fade, so the difference between the two is something you see rather than read.

> The three auras are an editorial reading. The notes are exactly as the house lists them.

The rest of the collection hangs like portraits on a wall, each bottle on a tone taken from its fragrance family. Engraving shows your initials on the real bottle before you commit to them.

## The details

- **One red.** Crimson appears only where the scent itself does, so the colour carries meaning.
- **Real prices, dated.** Every Singapore price comes from the house's own site, with the date it was checked.
- **Honest by default.** A first-visit notice explains that this is an unofficial concept, and checkout is a demonstration.
